Why notebookLM?
 NotebookLM is better than Copilot or plain ChatGPT when you want a “research workspace” that is tightly grounded in your own documents and large knowledge bases, with strong citations and persistent project context.  Grounded in your own sourcesNotebookLM’s core design is: you create notebooks, attach many sources (PDFs, docs, web pages, YouTube, etc.), and the model always answers based on those sources, with inline citations and links back into the document.  ChatGPT and Copilot can use uploaded files, but they are primarily general assistants; they are more likely to mix in outside knowledge or vary answers, while NotebookLM stays constrained to the notebook content and gives consistent, source-backed responses.  

ChatGPT and Copilot are optimized for single chats or coding sessions; they have strong context windows but do not treat a whole multi-file corpus as a stable “mini-model” tied to one project in the same way.
